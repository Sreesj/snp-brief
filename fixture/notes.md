# The demo list

`snps.txt` is the demo list (20 SNPs), chosen by the team to cover pathogenic, protective, trait, pharmacogenomic and poorly characterised variants, plus two non-SNV variants (small deletions) that test the edges of the pipeline. `held-out.txt` is one extra id (rs34637584, LRRK2 G2019S) that is not in the list and is run once at the end.

The notes below say why a variant is a useful test case. They are not fetched claims or frequencies; the briefs report those from the sources. Where the team has not written a note yet, the generated brief is the description.
